What changed from the first draft

  • Voice cut from 104 words (about 42 s) to 54 words (about 21.6 s), so it fits 30 s with pauses.
  • The team moved to the end, with 4 s on screen. Five names and roles in 1.5 s could not be read. The opening is now a single question that hooks the viewer.
  • One name everywhere: Nobotara71 (as on the website's logo), spoken "Nobotara Seventy-One". "Echoes Beyond Earth" is removed.
  • Each mission gets one action the game really has: the laser pulse, the Golden Record, the plan puzzle. "Original architecture reconstruction" is replaced by "the plan".
  • The three-missions shot uses Mission 03's real closing scene instead of a composited one.
  • The chatbot is called SpaceAI, as on the site.
  • The final 28-word on-screen statement is replaced by the spoken legacy line. It is still good text for the written project description.

Production notes

  • No NASA logo (the "meatball" or the "worm"). NASA restricts its use, and it can suggest endorsement. Credit sources in text, such as science.nasa.gov, and keep "Thank you, NASA".
  • AI disclosure. The game's narration is AI-generated (each panel says AI VOICE), and some site images are AI-made. If the video's voice-over is AI too, say so in the submission. Check the 2026 Space Apps rules on declaring AI use and on video length.
