NASA/JPL models from NASA 3D Resources. Free to use with credit.

perseverance-lite.glb
The NASA Perseverance rover, reduced for the game: about 55,000 faces, 512-pixel textures, normal maps kept, Draco compressed.
The Jezero scene loads it.
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-2020-perseverance-rover/

mars-approach.glb
NASA Mars globe shown at the start of Mission 3, with Jezero facing the camera. Hidden once the rover view begins.

mars-lite.glb
A smaller Mars globe, shown beside the Moon and Voyager 1 in the last shot of Mission 3.

The Blender files and full-size originals these were made from are in model-sources/nasa/ at the top of the repository.
They are kept out of public/ so they are not shipped with the game.

Credit: NASA/Jet Propulsion Laboratory
