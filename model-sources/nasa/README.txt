Working files for the NASA models used in Mission 3. Nothing here is loaded by the game or copied into the build.

perseverance.glb, perseverance.blend
The full NASA Perseverance model.
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-2020-perseverance-rover/

perseverance-lite.blend (and its .blend1 backup)
The reduced rover. Exported to public/models/nasa/perseverance-lite.glb.

mars-lite.blend, mars-color.jpg
The small Mars globe and its colour map. Exported to public/models/nasa/mars-lite.glb.

sample-tube.stl
NASA's Mars 2020 sample tube. Not used yet; the tubes in the game are built in code.
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-2020-sample-tube/
